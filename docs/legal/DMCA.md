# Copyright / IP Takedown Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-28

Insurance 2026 respects intellectual property rights and responds to valid copyright complaints.

## How to Submit a Notice

Send notices to: dmca@your-domain.example

A valid notice should include:
1. Identification of the copyrighted work.
2. Identification of the allegedly infringing material (exact URL/location).
3. Your contact details.
4. A good-faith statement that use is unauthorized.
5. A statement under penalty of perjury that your claim is accurate and you are authorized to act.
6. Your signature (physical or electronic).

## Counter-Notice

If you believe material was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice including legal basis and consent to jurisdiction as required by applicable law.

## Repeat Infringer Policy

We may suspend or terminate repeat infringers where legally and operationally appropriate.

## Good-Faith Requirement

Fraudulent notices or abusive takedown requests may be rejected and reported.

# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-28

This Privacy Policy explains how Insurance 2026 collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data.

## 1. Data We Collect

- Account and profile data you provide.
- Transaction and request metadata (timestamps, statuses).
- Device and network data (IP, user-agent, security telemetry).
- Operational logs for reliability and abuse prevention.

## 2. Why We Process Data

- Service delivery and support.
- Security monitoring, fraud prevention, and abuse mitigation.
- Legal and regulatory compliance.
- Operational analytics and performance optimization.

## 3. Legal Basis

Processing is based on one or more of:
- Contractual necessity.
- Legitimate interests (service security and continuity).
- Legal obligations.
- Consent where required.

## 4. Data Sharing

We may share data only when necessary with:
- Infrastructure and processing providers.
- Compliance, legal, or regulatory authorities where required.
- Security responders in abuse/fraud investigations.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Data Retention

- Data is retained only as long as needed for service, legal, and security purposes.
- Retention windows may differ by data category and legal obligations.

## 6. Security Measures

We use layered controls including:
- Access controls and authorization boundaries.
- Encryption where applicable.
- Logging, monitoring, and incident response workflows.
- Dependency and vulnerability management.

## 7. International Transfers

If data is processed outside your jurisdiction, appropriate safeguards are applied as required by law.

## 8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:
- Access to your data.
- Correction of inaccurate data.
- Deletion or restriction where legally permissible.
- Objection to specific processing activities.

## 9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

If used, cookies are applied for session continuity, security, and functional service behavior. Optional analytics cookies should be disclosed separately where applicable.

## 10. Children

The Service is not intended for unlawful or unauthorized use by minors without proper legal authority.

We may update this Privacy Policy as systems and legal obligations evolve.

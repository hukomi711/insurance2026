# Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-28

This policy defines prohibited behavior when using Insurance 2026.

## Prohibited Activities

You must not:
- Commit fraud, phishing, social engineering, or impersonation.
- Submit unauthorized payment data or stolen credentials.
- Attempt account takeover, brute force, token theft, or session hijacking.
- Perform scraping, automated abuse, denial-of-service, or traffic flooding.
- Distribute malware, exploit code, or harmful payloads.
- Upload or transmit infringing, illegal, or harmful content.
- Violate laws, regulations, or third-party rights.

## Security and Enforcement

- We actively monitor for abuse and suspicious activity.
- We may throttle, block, suspend, or terminate abusive traffic/accounts.
- We may preserve and disclose relevant records for legal/security obligations.

## Reporting Abuse

Send abuse reports with evidence to: abuse@your-domain.example

Include:
- Timestamp and timezone
- URLs/endpoints involved
- Screenshots/log excerpts
- Source IP (if available)
- Contact for follow-up
